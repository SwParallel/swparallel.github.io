I am a Senior student at **EECS, Peking University**, finishing my undergraduate studies soon. Inspired by the college’s interdisciplinary education, my research centers on Computer Vision, video/image generation and image quality assessment.

# Research Interests
My research mainly targets coherent video generation and diffusion model optimization:
* **Video Colorization & Temporal Consistency:** Optical flow guided diffusion frameworks to eliminate frame flicker for grayscale video recoloring.
* **Image & Video Quality Assessment:** Subjective and objective evaluation for compressed images.

# Education
* **B.S. in Informatics and Computation Science**, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EECS), Peking University, 2022 - Present, Peking University, 2022 - Present

# Beyond Research
Outside research, I am passionate about a cappella music and serve as the director of **Paca Vocal Group**. I spend plenty of time arranging vocal harmony, rehearsing performances and managing group operations.

I deeply enjoy the layered, coordinated sound of unaccompanied singing, and the teamwork required for ensemble performances gives me a distinct way to think about consistency and harmony—ideas that also resonate with my research.
